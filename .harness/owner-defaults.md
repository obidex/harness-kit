# Harness Kit · owner defaults

> After `core.md`. Records: `.harness/catalogue/owner-defaults.md`. Projects only tighten these,
> by a profile exception naming a decision.

- **O01 Act within authorization.** Do the work yourself. Ask the owner only for what stays
  reserved: an account, token or app; a payment; a physical step or one on his machine; a secret; a
  paid or owner-only setting; a gate or design approval; a refused action; his decision.
- **O02 One batch.** Everything needed from him goes in ONE `WAITING FOR YOU` message, at most a few
  steps, tedious part first. Each step is exact: the link, the button, the text to paste, what he
  sees when it worked. Never "configure X". What a session can detect, he never confirms. Relative
  times only. A new batch replaces the open one.
- **O03 Decisions.** A decision comes as A/B (at most C), a one-line trade-off each and the
  recommendation; he answers with a letter. Inside an approved plan, take the recommendation
  yourself and report it.
- **O04 Secrets.** Secrets go into GitHub or the tool itself, never into chat. Check a secret by
  name, never by reading it. A secret seen outside its store is burned: report it for rotation.
- **O05 His approval is his own act.** No agent posts, reviews, reacts or merges in place of his
  approval.
- **O06 Model roles.** Plain status uses existing checks, never an extra turn. Haiku at low effort
  reads logs and summarizes, only when that saves real work; never writes money, stock or permission
  code, never reviews. Building uses the smallest reliable model (Sonnet for clear, bounded work);
  Opus for architecture, accounting, security and hard or risky work. Set per thread, never
  switched; good work is never redone for its model.
- **O07 Verification by risk.** Independent review scales with risk (C10), Opus preferred for tier
  3. Optional reviewers are requested once and never waited on; each of their findings ends FIXED,
  TRACKED or DISMISSED with evidence.
- **O08 Short headline reports.** Status word and answer first; terse, plain, decision-first; no
  narrated process.
- **O09 No Claude attribution.** Commits are authored as the owner; squash bodies carry no
  `Co-authored-by`; no "Generated by" footers anywhere. `harness-scrub.yml` enforces it (K006).
- **O10 Alerts.** One bot, one Telegram group, every sender through `notify.mjs`
  (`.harness/alerts.md`): "Needs you" only for action required or outages; quiet 23-08 Damascus;
  every PROBLEM ends RESOLVED; never success.
- **O11 The owner is not a merge gate.** He decides and may veto; threads merge their own PRs on the
  gates (C12).
- **O12 Read for meaning.** When two readings lead to different irreversible actions, ask one yes/no
  in the batch; otherwise act on the meaning.
- **O13 Metered resources need notice.** Nothing using hosted minutes, a paid service, storage or
  another metered resource is added or increased before the owner hears what and an estimate.
  Schedules run no more often than needed (an event or daily check beats hourly polling); say which
  minutes are unmetered.
- **O14 Cross-project requests go through the inbox.** Work for another project is an `inbox` issue
  in the repo that does it, with the `inbox` skill's fields, including the owner decision or
  delegation covering it; it proceeds only once that cover is verified and in scope (citing a
  decision never expands it). State and evidence stay on the issue.
- **O15 Never a relay.** No automation, routine or thread asks the owner to wake, nudge or relay
  between sessions. What cannot reach a session records its state for the next scheduled check: at
  most a daily-summary line, never "Needs you".
- **O16 Close threads.** A thread is resolved in the turn it reports DONE; anything due later is a
  coordinator's scheduled check-in, never an idle thread. WAITING FOR YOU only for true owner steps.
- **O17 Owner effort.** Before an owner step goes out, try in order an API or token, a pre-filled
  link, a guided script, then bundling; send what is left with its action count and minutes.
