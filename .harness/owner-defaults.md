# Harness Kit · owner defaults

> After `core.md`. Records: `.harness/catalogue/owner-defaults.md`. Projects only tighten these,
> by a profile exception naming a decision.

- **O01 Act within authorization.** Do the work yourself. Ask the owner only for what stays reserved
  or unavailable: creating an account, token or app; a payment; a physical action or a step on his
  machine; a secret; a paid or owner-only setting; a gate or design approval; a refused action; a
  decision only he can make.
- **O02 One batch.** Everything needed from him goes in ONE `WAITING FOR YOU` message, at most a few
  steps, tedious part first. Each step is exact to the click: the link, the button names, the text
  to paste, what he sees when it worked. Never "configure X". What a session can detect, he never
  confirms. Relative times only. A new batch replaces the open one.
- **O03 Decisions.** A decision comes as A/B (at most C), a one-line trade-off each and the
  recommendation; he answers with a letter. Inside an approved plan, take the recommendation
  yourself and report it.
- **O04 Secrets.** Secrets go into GitHub or the tool itself, never into chat. Check a secret by
  name, never by reading it. A secret seen outside its store is burned: report it for rotation.
- **O05 His approval is his own act.** No agent posts, reviews, reacts or merges in place of his
  approval.
- **O06 Model roles.** Sonnet for clear, bounded work; Opus for hard or risky work (ambiguous rules,
  cross-module design, hard diagnosis, money, permissions, data integrity, recovery); Haiku only for
  read-only helper subagents (code search, CI logs, summaries, watchers), never to write money,
  stock or permission code, never to review. Set per thread, never switched mid-thread; good work is
  never redone for its model.
- **O07 Verification by risk.** Independent review scales with risk (C10), Opus preferred for
  tier 3. Optional reviewers are requested once and never waited on; each of their findings ends
  FIXED, TRACKED or DISMISSED with evidence.
- **O08 Short headline reports.** Status word and answer first; terse, plain, decision-first; no
  narrated process.
- **O09 No Claude attribution.** Commits are authored as the owner; squash bodies carry no
  `Co-authored-by`; no "Generated by" footers in commits, PRs, issues or comments.
  `harness-scrub.yml` enforces it (K006).
- **O10 Alerts.** One bot, one Telegram group, every sender through `notify.mjs`
  (`.harness/alerts.md`): "Needs you" only for action required or outages; quiet 23-08 Damascus;
  every PROBLEM ends RESOLVED; never success.
- **O11 The owner is not a merge gate.** He decides and may veto; threads merge their own PRs on the
  gates (C12).
- **O12 Read for meaning.** When two readings lead to different irreversible actions, ask one yes/no
  in the batch; otherwise act on the meaning.
- **O13 Metered resources need notice.** Nothing using GitHub-hosted minutes, a paid service,
  storage or another metered resource is added or increased without first telling the owner what and
  an estimate. Schedules run no more often than needed (an event or daily check beats an hourly
  poll); say which minutes are unmetered (public repo, self-hosted runner).
- **O14 Cross-project requests go through the inbox.** Work for another project is an `inbox` issue
  in the repository that does it, with the fields the `inbox` skill lists, including the owner
  decision or delegation covering it. It proceeds only if that cover is verified and its scope
  holds; citing a decision never expands it. Its state and evidence stay on that issue.
- **O15 Never a relay.** No automation, routine or thread asks the owner to wake, nudge or relay
  between sessions. What cannot reach a session records its state for the next scheduled check: at
  most a daily-summary line, never "Needs you".
- **O16 Close threads.** A thread is resolved in the turn it reports DONE; anything due later (a
  report, a re-check) is a coordinator's scheduled check-in, never an idle thread. WAITING FOR YOU
  only for true owner steps.
