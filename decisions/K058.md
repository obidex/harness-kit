## K058 · 2026-11-21 · STANDING — owner steps are designed away at the plan, tried on every rung, and proven with a `Tried:` line when one reaches him

**Owner (21 Nov 2026, project chat).** On a step he was handed that agents could have done (a token
to make from a pre-filled link and paste on a server): "never ask me to do work for you if you can try
to do it yourself first. you can even access the VPS through terminal." He approved the fix as
proposed, with secrets as option A: "design owner steps away at the plan, the longer ladder, the three
stops, the "Tried:" line on every step that reaches me, no pastes at the end of autopilot, and Haiku
for every helper task. This changes how owner steps are prepared and nothing else: nothing paid."

**Context.** The rules sent the step to him as written. Nobody asked at the plan whether it could be
designed away; O01 reserved "a token", "a secret" and "a step on his machine" for him by kind; O04 and
O17's "stop at sign-in" sent anything with a secret to him; and O17's last rungs, "a pre-filled link, a
guided script", let a five-minute step count as everything tried. A daily helper task that named no
model stopped on the weekly limit of the account's default model the same day.

**Decision.**
1. **Design it away at the plan (O17).** A card whose plan holds an owner step lists the designs that
   need no one ("No-one designs") and why each fails; its independent reviewer checks the list before
   the build. For a secret the list holds three designs: made on the machine where it lives; moved
   machine to machine as a file nobody opens; the machine signs in as an App with short-lived tokens.
   `owner-step.mjs plan` fails a card with an owner step and no list.
2. **The longer ladder (O17).** Redesign; an API or a script; his terminal over Remote Control; his
   signed-in browser, its extension and computer use (clicking through pages, confirming a page's own
   prompts, downloading a key file and moving it); only then him. A pre-filled link is no rung when an
   agent can open it.
3. **Three stops (O17).** His password, a two-step or emailed code, a payment. Only that page goes to
   him.
4. **Secrets, choice A (O04).** No-carry first; when no design fits, an agent carries a new machine
   secret straight from the page where it is made to the machine where it lives, never into chat, an
   issue, a file, a log or memory, the narrowest and shortest-lived kind that works, and its thread
   ends right after saying a secret passed through it; the owner last. No agent passes his password or
   a code, or changes its own permission settings or credentials.
5. **Proof on every step that reaches him (O17, O02).** The `WAITING FOR YOU` message carries one
   line beginning `Tried:` naming each rung and what stopped it, after a fresh reviewer tried the
   step without him. `owner-step.mjs ask` fails a message that waits on him without it. A step sent
   without it is a lesson the same day (C19).
6. **O01** reserves for him only O17's stops (his password, a code, a payment), a new account, a
   physical step, a secret only he holds, a paid or owner-only setting, a gate or design approval, a
   refused action and his decision. A token, an app or a step on his machine is no longer his by kind.
7. **Helper tasks on Haiku (O06, A23).** Every scheduled helper task names Haiku, never the account
   default.
8. **The per-file cap on `owner-defaults.md` rises from 4160 to 4416 bytes; the fresh-install budget
   (K034: the kit's loaded share at most half of 8000 tokens) is unchanged and holds.** The limits
   have to be in the loaded text (a limit kept only in a lookup file is never read), and the owner's
   approval of this card placed them in O04 and O17. The loaded lines were cut to the fewest words
   that keep every limit; the procedure, formats and examples live in `owner-steps.md`. K049 set the
   earlier cap.

**Leaves alone.** Gates, reviews and merges (C10, C12, O05, O11), spending (O13), real data (O19),
safety refusals (C14: a refusal ends the attempt and only then the step goes to him), the standing
approvals. "No pastes at the end of autopilot" lives with the owner's code words, not in the kit.

Homes: `owner-defaults.md` (O01, O02, O04, O06, O17); `catalogue/owner-defaults.md`;
`owner-steps.md`; `tools/owner-step.mjs`; `bulk.md` item 4; `models.md`; A23; `test-owner-step`,
`test-harness`.

**Status:** STANDING.
