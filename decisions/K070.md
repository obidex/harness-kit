## K070 · 2026-11-26 · STANDING — agents report their own friction; read-only lookups in a small worker; the waiting share; a shorter core; the runbook linter

**Asked.** A strategist request on the owner's board (26 Nov 2026), five kit asks drawn from Uber's public
write-up "Skills at Enterprise Scale", under the owner's approval of the same night ("I approve all the
improvements you want to make to our workflow and all the upgrades to whatever, all approved"; recorded on
the board, 26 Nov 2026, 00:34 Damascus). Technical, kit-internal work: no money, no design. Not taken, as
the request says: a marketplace, tiers, an installer, per-skill dashboards.

**What was found.**
- Nothing collected an agent's own friction: a broken flag or a wrong step was worked around and lost, or
  told to the owner.
- A thread's most frequent reads (its state note, its queue, a failed log) went through its own model,
  in its full context, every time.
- The waiting, measured from one busy day's session records (25 Nov 2026: 20 threads of one project,
  140 wakes, use weighted by token price): 91 wakes (37% of use) were started by a background task
  finishing, and 57 wakes ended within four model calls (9% of use), mostly a thread noting a task whose
  result had already reached it (a subagent's hand-back is followed by its task notice) and then ending.
  No polling loop was found in the sample read. Each such wake still re-reads the whole context, about
  300k tokens on a busy thread.
- Of the always-loaded files, `core.md` (8,703 of 8,704 bytes) had room to shrink: much of its text says
  again what its catalogue rows already record. `owner-defaults.md` (4,541) did not: a cut by a third
  dropped owner-decided wording that `test-harness` pins word for word (O01, O04, O06, O15, O17, O19, from
  K046, K047, K058), and a test is never weakened (C08). A project's own rulebook is that project's (O14).
- The kit's two skills and its runbooks had no shape check; neither had the owner's plugin skills.

**Decision.**
1. **Papercuts (C28).** A thread that a tool, skill, rule or instruction fights files one short report
   with `papercut.mjs file` (what, tried, expected, happened, fix; one line each, at most 400 characters,
   secret-shaped text refused) and carries on. Reports go to one private issue per project, labelled
   `papercut` (profile `papercuts`, or the repository's own, opened by the first report); a public
   repository is refused. The tool keeps the issue body: a table grouped by what was reported and a count
   line a status page can read. Weekly, by event and not by a schedule: a coordinator run that sees
   `papercut.mjs due` print DUE posts one digest across projects and files cards for the top three
   (`papercuts.md`, the run's contract in `wake.md`).
2. **Read-only lookups (`lookups.md`).** The state note and a failed log are read by a Haiku worker at
   low effort with a fixed brief and a line limit; the queue and the papercut table by script. The
   summary is data; the thread opens the source only for the item it acts on.
3. **A wake costs the whole context (adapter A25).** Work the next step needs runs in the foreground; a
   notice for work whose result already arrived ends its turn with no tool call; CI and reviews are
   waited on by PR event (A06) or one reminder, an unavoidable poll in a Haiku worker. The after-count is
   the same measure on a busy day after this release.
4. **A shorter core.** `core.md` 8,703 to 6,561 bytes (the editable text, outside C27's quoted
   paragraphs, cut by about a third), every rule kept with its record, every pinned phrase kept, and C28
   added. `owner-defaults.md` unchanged (above). The replay set, 31 tasks, decides: the result for this
   version must hold the floor against 0.59.0's 31 of 31.
5. **The linter's three checks (`steps.mjs`).** Every `SKILL.md`, and every file marked
   `<!-- runbook -->`, has numbered steps; each step a command (or a stated `No command:` with the reason)
   and a `Check:`; and a failure table whose every fix says what to do, never only "retry". The kit's own
   skills and runbooks pass, and `test-steps` keeps them passing.

**Record.** `.harness/core.md` (C28 and the cut), its catalogue row; adapter A25 and its row;
