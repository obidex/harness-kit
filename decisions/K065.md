## K065 · 2026-11-25 · STANDING — a hand-off's marks count only from an account GitHub says may write, not by its association

**Asked.** harness-kit#88, found by the second reviewer on the hand-off change (K056, 0.50.0) and made
ready by the board sort of 25 Nov 2026. `wake.mjs` counted a hand-off receipt, its failed start and a
run's "started" record when the comment's `author_association` was OWNER, MEMBER or COLLABORATOR. That
field is no write check: MEMBER is any member of the owning organization, whatever their access to the
repository, and COLLABORATOR includes outside collaborators with read or triage only. On a public
repository owned by an organization, such an account could use a cap up, build three receipt and fail
pairs so the job sets `wake-paused`, or spend a label start with a "started" record. It could not start a
run. A repository owned by a user account has no members and only writing collaborators, so nothing
the kit runs on today was exposed.

**Decision.**
1. **The owner counts as before.** An author GitHub lists as OWNER needs no further read.
2. **A member or collaborator is asked.** For each such author of a hand-off receipt, a hand-off's failed
   start or a run's "started" record, the tools read `GET /repos/{repo}/collaborators/{login}/permission`
   once (per author, per process) and count the mark only for write, maintain or admin (`role_name`, else
   the older `permission`). No collaborator (404) is no writer. Any other refusal stops the decision, as a
   cut-off comment listing does: nothing is decided on an unread count.
3. **Everything else is unchanged.** A stranger's comment, a forged job receipt and an App's
   "started" record are judged as before; `ledger` stays pure and takes the writers as an input
   (`writersOf` reads them first).

**Record.** `.harness/tools/wake.mjs` (`writersOf`, `askFor`, `mayWrite`, `ledger`, `writer`, `usedSince`,
`started`), `.harness/wake.md` "Counts and stops"; checks in `tools/test-wake.mjs`. Narrows K056 and K057's
"an account that may write"; changes no cap, stop or label.

**Status:** STANDING.
