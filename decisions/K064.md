## K064 · 2026-11-25 · STANDING — the job and a hand-off keep or yield a start by one rule, so a slow job receipt cannot give two runs

**Asked.** harness-kit#90, tracked from the review of #89 (K057, 0.51.0) and made ready by the board sort
of 25 Nov 2026. With `WAKE_START=label`, the job decides with no claim on. Before its receipt lands, a
hand-off (K056) decides too, claims, writes its receipt, keeps its start after its settle time (8 s) and
calls `fire_trigger`. The job's receipt lands more than 8 s later; the job clears the claim and sets it
again, which fires the label trigger, and `wake.mjs started` judges the job's receipt and answers OK:
two runs work. The fix tried in #89 (the job backs off from any claim that appeared after its decision)
stalled the common same-moment order: the hand-off yields to the job's receipt, the job backs off, no
run works and the claim waits 6 hours. Both sides need one rule.

**Decision.**
1. **`seen` on every receipt.** The job's receipt, like the hand-off's (K057), names the newest receipt
   its decision read. A hand-off receipt also names the rule it follows (`"rule":"K064"`).
2. **One rule, `jobBacksOff(h, j)`.** For a hand-off receipt `h` that the job's receipt `j` had not read:
   the hand-off keeps the start, and the job backs off, when the hand-off had read `j` itself, or `j`
   came more than the settle time after `h` (by then the hand-off has read the receipts and started its
   run). Otherwise the hand-off yields, as K056 says. Judged from receipt ids and GitHub's times only, so
   the job, the hand-off (`keepsStart`) and `started` reach the same answer whatever order they read in.
3. **The job backs off** right after writing its receipt, in both start modes: it touches no claim (it
   is the hand-off's run's), sets no label, makes no call, writes a note (`<!-- wake-backoff -->`, which
   no count reads) on the STATE issue and tells the woken issue; the `wake` label waits for that run.
   Its receipt still counts in the caps and is not a failed start.
4. **`started` passes over a backed-off job receipt** as it passes over a hand-off that yielded, judged
   from the receipts alone, so a run that asks before the job's note lands is refused too. `--failed`
   still releases a hand-off start that a job receipt backed off from.
5. **Old receipts keep the old behaviour.** A job receipt without `seen` or a hand-off receipt without
   the rule never makes the job back off, so a project whose job and senders are on different kit
   versions behaves exactly as before (at worst the old race), never a new stall.

**Not decided here.** A lagging repository-wide comment listing (a receipt written inside the settle
time but not yet listed) is unchanged and tracked on harness-kit#98.

**Record.** `.harness/tools/wake.mjs` (`jobBacksOff`, `keepsStart`, `startOk`, `runFire`, `runHandoff`),
`.harness/wake.md` "The job and a hand-off: one rule"; checks in `tools/test-wake.mjs` (both orders, both
start modes, `started`, `--failed`). Extends K056 and K057; changes no cap, stop or label.

**Status:** STANDING.
