## K055 · 2026-11-20 · STANDING — the second reviewer is installed everywhere and off; only the owner's command switches it on, until a time he names

**Owner (20 Nov 2026, relayed card and project chat).** A project reported its second reviewer as "on".
He answered that the set-up should be in every project, and: "it's good to spend the allowance we have
now but don't make it a rule to spend it all the time. just spend it this time on my command." He
approved the card with: "off by default, with one command to switch it on until a date and off again
... It is never a merge gate, and nothing may switch it on except my own command", and asked for one
full check of the code that already exists in his two largest projects.

**Context.** K054 made the reviewer inert until its federation rule's variable was set, and from then
on it reviewed every pull request for good. That turned a set-up step into a standing spend, and an
agent's routine pass could complete the set-up and so switch it on.

**Decision.**
1. **Set-up and switch are two things.** The four identifiers (K054) are the set-up and stay in place.
   The switch is one more Actions variable, `REVIEW_UNTIL`: a date and time with its offset. The
   reviewer runs only while that time is ahead and no more than 45 days away. Not set, unreadable,
   passed or further ahead is off. No value means "on for good", and the end time switches it off
   without anyone coming back.
2. **Only the owner's command sets it**: "reviewer on until <date>" or "reviewer off". A session
   carries the command out, in the repositories he names, and reports the value. No rule, schedule,
   routine or agent pass sets, extends or re-creates the variable. A status says "installed, off" or
   "on until <time>".
3. **A project taking this version is switched off by it** until his command: that is the default he
   asked for, and it brings every earlier "on" in line.
4. **One full check of existing code, on his command.** `harness-sweep.yml` with `sweep.mjs`: started
   by hand from the default branch, never by a schedule or an event, and only inside the window of
   point 1. Findings become issues graded blocker, major or minor; one record issue carries the
   numbers. Private repositories only: on a public one it refuses, since its issues would publish
   defects in code that is in use. Caps in code ($30 a check, 250 areas, 320 minutes a run); its own federation rule, pinned
   to that file and to a run started by hand. An estimate that sends nothing is the default mode.
5. **Still advisory** (K054 point 2, unchanged): neither the reviewer nor the full check is ever a
   required check, an approval or a merge gate.
6. **What it reads is data, and less than everything.** A symbolic link is never followed, a file
   that may hold a secret is never sent, nothing of the repository is run.

**Supersedes** K054 point 4's "the workflow is inert until the rule's variable is set" (it is now
inert until the switch is open as well) and its opening "reads every pull request": every pull
request while switched on. The rest of K054 stands.

Homes: `tools/review.mjs` (`switchOf`); `tools/sweep.mjs`; `templates/workflows/harness-review.yml`,
`templates/workflows/harness-sweep.yml`; `review.md`; `test-review`, `test-sweep`.

**Status:** STANDING.
