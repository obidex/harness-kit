## K081 · 2026-11-27 · STANDING — the reviewer may ask another family through OpenRouter, only endpoints that keep and train on nothing, and its noise is counted

**Asked.** A strategist's card on the owner's board (27 Nov 2026), on the owner's words that Claude reviewing Claude's
own work is expensive and weak value: every project gets a non-Claude reviewer before the Claude one ends, on
OpenRouter (no switch of provider), with no stored key where a keyless way exists, else one key with a monthly limit;
only endpoints that do not train on requests; and, for each reviewer, the comments dismissed counted against those
that led to a fix.

**Found.** OpenRouter offers keyless sign-in by workload identity federation, but only on its paid Business plan;
the owner's account is a personal one, and buying a plan is spending. The account already sends every model group to
endpoints that keep nothing or keep only for abuse checks, and every training switch is off; those are switches a
person can change, so the reviewer should not depend on them. GLM 5.3's endpoints on OpenRouter range from $0.07 to
$2.80 a million input tokens, so a price written in code holds only with a ceiling on each call. The kit reviewer's
comments ask each point to end FIXED, TRACKED or DISMISSED, but in free text no tool can count.

**Decision.**
1. **A provider setting.** `provider` in `.github/harness-review.json` (or `REVIEW_PROVIDER`): `anthropic`, the
   default, or `openrouter`. Unknown is off. The switch (K055), the settings file (K060), the caps and the one
   comment are unchanged; the code checks and the staff guide stay on Claude.
2. **GLM 5.3 on OpenRouter** on every path, until a trial on the projects' own past bugs shows a stronger model
   catches more serious defects on high-risk paths. Models and prices change only by a kit release.
3. **One key, not a keyless sign-in**, because the keyless way needs a paid plan: the repository secret
   `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, a key for the reviewer alone with its own monthly limit as the hard stop. It reaches only
   the review step's environment. It is the only secret the workflow reads, and a test holds that.
4. **Data and price per call**: `zdr: true`, `data_collection: "deny"` and `max_price` equal to the price counted,
   so neither a privacy switch nor a dearer endpoint can change what is sent where or what it costs.
5. **Noise.** The comment asks the owning thread for one tally line; `review.mjs noise` counts per model the points
   raised and their ends, with the share dismissed and the share fixed.

**Not done, and why.** No keyless sign-in (a paid plan). No stronger model on risky paths yet (the trial is the
ERP's, roadmap#221). The noise count covers this reviewer only; the ERP's own reviewers are counted on its card.
